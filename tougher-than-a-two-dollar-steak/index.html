Tougher Than A Two Dollar Steak

I don't always know what to say here.

What to muse about.

When I sits.

To say and muse.

 So I'm going to say something to all the men out there.

Without being all gender generalized, I want to say men need to hear this more than women.

Because women tend to do the right thing.

At least in this context.

Fellas, if you can sense something is wrong with your health, get it checked out.

This message is especially for those who believe they are invincible.

You have trusted your gut your entire life.

And if your gut is telling you something isn't right.

Get it checked out.

There are no fields I hate vehemently dislike more than fields where they act as if you work for them, but really you're the ones who are in charge. 

When it comes to your health and wellbeing, you are in charge.

I got a little silly after surviving a big thing that I let myself go, because I was just so thankful to be here.

But like all (soon to be) classic cars, you gotta put in a lot of work to keep it a classic.

Otherwise they throw it in a scrap metal heap.

Oh, and to any stubborn women out there, please trust your gut, too.

I was poked and prodded hundreds of times.

To the point where they couldn't find any veins anymore left to poke.

So I said no more.

And when things got bad, I waited too long to go back to ask for help.

I vehemently dislike to generalize again, but men seem to do that.

I mused (yesterday?) about a hospice doctor who said many of his patients "discovered" at the very end that their life was meant to experience all that life has to offer.

That it was perfect that way.

Maybe holistic is another word we could put on it.

Remember that show Little House on the Prairie?

Well, our family started watching it while I was healing.

I couldn't watch because I realized almost every episode was a sad one.

Most times there'd be a happy ending, with the Ingalls putting on a pot of fresh coffee in the middle of the night.

But I couldn't watch it.

I felt I was living it, to some weird extent.

One of the things I loved about the show was the way they would help others.

Charles would pitch it like it was the other person who was doing him the favor.

"Boy, we sure did make too much soup, we sure would be sad to let any of it spoil."

So listen.

What I'm saying is this.

And I know I'm all over the place but that's just who I am.

Remember Rocky running up those steps?

Iconic.

Pinnacle moment.

But guess what?

He still has to fight the championship fight.

Sometimes, we celebrate the stairs moments a little too much.

We all love goosebump moments and training montages.

When we really need to wrap our hands for the next, real, fight.

And while I can't give you a cufflink from Rocky Marciano, I can give you this:

"Get up, you son of a good woman, 'cause Alex loves you."

How much fight you got left in you, champ?

I know you got a lot.

But even world champions need to rest and heal in between fights.

So heal up.

Until it's time to ring the bell.

Get up.

Until it's time to go.
