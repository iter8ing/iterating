Different Old, Different Old

What could I tell you, that hasn't been told a bizillion times before?

...

Much of life is fraught with things you really don't think about until it happens to you or someone close to you.

I say fraught but I could just as easily say blessed.

It doesn't feel like one at the time, but if you can get through it (and I know you can), you will often view it as a learning lesson.

A wissmart moment.

Who wants to be wisdumb?

No, no, the goal is to be wissmart.

Another thing I'll tell you is that everything has a price.

Not always fiat.

But always paid in full.

If not immediately, then later.

I suppose people call it karma.

I sometimes do.

These aren't particularly novel ideas, hmm?

How about this:

If your life was a movie, would you pay to watch it?

Would you spend the hour and forty to sit and see?

What would be in the film?

What would you make up?

I hope you wouldn't make anything up, but they do, you know.

They make a movie about someone who really lived and sometimes make stuff up.

Why?

To make the life more interesting.

Ain't that something?

"Based on a true story."

Means kinda true.

Some parts not so much.

In a good story, there is tension.

Could be internal.

Could be ex.

But it requires the character to change.

It requires what they call an inciting incident.

A usually crazy event that disrupts one's normal routine.

Introducing the conflict.

And the main character then has to make a choice.

He has to take action that differs wildly from his typical activities.

This HAS to happen.

In a good story.

It HAS to.

A story about a guy who hits 500 foot homers every time he steps up to the plate would be a boring story.

It would!

Unless he got bonked on the head and went blind.

And spent five years learning to hit by echo location.

What I'm trying to say is if you're going through it, remember it's part of the story.

And if you haven't gone through it yet, oooh baby, do you got it coming.

But remember:

Every story is a love story.

And every good story has to have a litlle (lot) of chaos.

And as much as I just want to live in peace and just be left alone (with my tribe), I realize that's just a dreamland.

It's time to act.

So, I ask, how's life?

And, more importantly, what are you going to do about it?

Action is expensive.

In costs.

Sweat.

Heartbeats.

Money.

Time.

But the alternative is to ignore it.

To do nothing.

And let me tell you something, that just makes for a greater conflict down the line.

Believe me, I've lived it.

Am living it.

All because I didn't want to act.

Because I felt it was opening Pandora's Box.

But the the truth is, the longer I waited, the bigger the box became.

So open the box, already.

Your life could use a little action.

C'mon, admit it.

Let's get a little crazy and tell a better story.

Your story.

Love.
