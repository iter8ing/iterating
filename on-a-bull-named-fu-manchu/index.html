And he'd spend all day bragging about his family.

Just how much he loved them.

How accomplished they were.

Just a genuinely happy, decent, good man.

Who loved his family.

A little too much.

And I think, I think, that's the sweet spot.

And I smelled it.

My Lord, do I miss that man.
