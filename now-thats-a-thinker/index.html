Now That's A Thinker

I admit it.

I think a lot.

Now, some fools out there will try and convince you that thinking isn't action.

But it is.

It's an action.

Patience, planning, and strategy sometimes confuses those who just jump at the first shiny object then the next.

Then the next.

Squirrel!!!

🐿

Why are we doing this?

That's a fine question.

How are we doing this?

Finer.

What are we doing?

Best question yet.

Who and when are probably something to consider, too.

All require thinking.

Thinking is an action.

People in this hustle culture or whatever the heck we call it today just brag about how they did this or that but they never stopped to think.

They just kept doing this or that.

That or this.

Look how great I turned out, is their schtick.

But I think, they turned out terribly.

At least for me.

For me.

You do you.

I don't want what they have or they claim they got.

I seek something different.

I don't have many heroes, but they all seem to do a lot of contemplating.

Which is just a fancy word for thinking.

Alright stop.

Collaborate and listen.

What are you doing?

Right now.

Why are you doing it?

How are you doing?

Who are you doing it with and for?

When will you decide to do something else?

Do you think it's a good idea?

Do you like your answers?

What are you going to do about it?
