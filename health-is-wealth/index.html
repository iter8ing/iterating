Health Is Wealth

Would you believe I write more than I do just here?

I wouldn't have believed it if you told me that a year ago.

None of this is my finest work.

Just repetitions, iterations, towards better work.

I'm trying to make a "What does this make possible" moment.

Season, really.

I could stagnate.

I could wallow.

Or I could heal.

I could grow.

Tomorrow, I get something that old(ish) people get.

Something, I hope helps me.

A lot of these musings lately have been all about me.

And I suppose that's the whole point of this thing.

To help me get to where I want to get to.

If it helps you, fantastic.

Truly.

I'm a guy who survived something a lot of folks do not.

And I think I spent a little too much time being grateful.

You mean ignoring it and silently worrying.

And not enough time keeping up the maintenance.

I was just so tired of being poked and prodded.

But I've learned, it hasn't been about just me for a long time, now.

Maybe ever.

That there's always been a precious group of souls who want my tookus back to 110%, or as close to it as possible.

Not just laying there like Jabba.

But flying like Boba.

Wisen like Yoda.

So I open the box.

And hope it has just what I need.

Getting old(ish) is something else I tell you.

What a blessing.
