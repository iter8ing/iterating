Royal Ramble

So how's everyone doing out there?

You good?

I'm good enough to type a musing.

Although the quality I'll leave up to you.

Not that this all that, but I'm glad to be writing again.

The body is incredibly adaptable.

Perhaps too much so.

You can learn to live with a lot of crap.

You can learn to live with so much.

It can even begin to feel normal.

What is abnormal is now normal.

I have the distinct advantage of never being normal a day in my life.

So I'm used to this "being different" thing.

You might be, too.

At the end of the night, I'm a motivational speaker.

At least, I'd like to think so.

So I want to tell you what you're going through right now.

Good, bad, everything.

It'll pass.

It's just about getting through it.

My friend used to say "inch by inch."

Like a tortoise.

🐢

But it's really about getting through the next X seconds.

Getting through the next second, minute, hour, day, week.

You get the ⏳.

I hear Einstein said time's an illusion.

Past, present, and future.

Same thing.

Instead of now, there's this 4D cube that has everything that has ever happened and ever will happen.

To me, "science" has always been so much more wildly unbelievable than the idea that there's God.

Sure, it simplifies things.

But I like simple.

I was able to survive something that oofs folks.

But it came and comes with consequences.

I keep and bear them as the cost of survival.

Side effects, they sometimes call them.

But nothing side about them.

They seem main effects to me.

But listen, I don't write a negative musings column.

So I want you to listen.

Listen really close.

Consequence is the finest teacher.

And healing can be instant, but it is often nonlinear.

And yes, you can get used to the suck.

If you have to.

I hope I don't have to for long.

But I have been.

Action -> Consequence.

I learn lessons every day.

God (in his 4D cube) is the greatest teacher.
