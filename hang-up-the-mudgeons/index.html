Hang Up The Mudgeons

Reality checks sometimes bounce.

Sometimes, they clear.

There comes a time though.

When a curmudgeon's gotta hang up his mudgeons.

He's gotta just say thank you.

One I've lived and live but never knew there was a term for.

PTG.

Post Traumatic Growth.

Focus on the G.

Something sheety happens.

And you transform.

Into something, into someone, better.

Like a mother motherin' snake doc.

Now, I ain't perfect.

But I kinda thought I was.

I kinda did.

You kinda did.

Boy, did I get a reckoning.

To the bone.

Humble pie was served.

That sheet came on a big sheet.

Here's the weird part.

The pie tasted good.

This is some good pie!

Still ticking.

No tocking.

But I know that's just me fighting a ghost.

I want to tell you how great grateful I am, but it's hard to say.

What doesn't keel you, makes you stronger.

Embrace adversities.

Revel in chaos.
