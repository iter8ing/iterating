Autumnal

You would think, I think, that I wouldn't forget to do something I do (almost) everyday.

But I do.

I forget.

In my defense, I thought I already wrote today.

But I guess I didn't.

Hey, it happens.

I've been thinking a lot.

As much as I do.

About things that used to be made better.

It never was an outright topic, but it's been coming up in my brain.

Vehicles.

I don't want a touch screen to turn on the heat.

Baseball cards.

Vintage and 90s parallels.

Video games.

You used to be able to own them in physical form.

I don't know if it's nostalgic me.

Autumn does that you know.

Wakes up nostalgic me.

I'm up, I'm up! 

I suppose I never sound older than when I talk about the good old days.

And the truth is, as good as they were, I love now more than ever.

More than yesterdays.

This whole evolution thing is a lot for an old(ish) man.

Cars drive themselves.

Kinda cool.

Big bucks are entrenched in cardboard.

It's an alternative asset class!!!

And every dang thing is a subscription or freemium.

You can play for free, but if you want to be cool, you have to pay.

A lot.

Did you know that they used to sell audiobooks on CD?

What's a CD? Cowboys wide receiver?

Ahh, football season.

They give these college kids millions now!

Over the table instead of under!

I think I got a couple years eligibility left.

Go deep!
