It's All In Your Head

Folks, I have some strange news to share.

I no longer drink Coke Zero.

Or any caffeine for that matter.

Going cold turkey (weird term) on the Zero hasn't been that difficult.

I'm sure I had withdrawal symptoms but they kinda faded in the background to all my other stuff I was going through.

It's crazy to say, but I don't miss it.

But guess what?

Chicken butt.

I also no longer eat Doritos.

Just went cold chicken on that, too.

Just cold poultrying everything.

It occurred to me that maybe I had all these issues because I was giving myself terrible fuel.

Low gas, no travel.

You know, the kinda fuel so low that you don't find it at most gas stations.

I wish I could tell you I'm a changed man because of the diet change.

And well, I am a changed man, but I'm not sure what has helped that most.

My fingers are no longer coated in a delicious powdered orange. 

My jaw is more relaxed.

And I'm eating food that's more nutritious.

And you know what?

I like drinking water.

We are creatures of routine.

So highly adaptable.

It amazes me that I have Coke Zero and Doritos around and I'm not tempted to eat it.

Not even a little.

Because I feel better without it.

Ain't that something?

Something I did everyday, multiple times a day, almost every meal.

I no longer do.

Just one day, after some considerable nudging, I stopped.

Now, I'm not expecting a standing ovation to stop drinking soda and eating tortilla chips, but I'll give myself one.

Take care of yourselves out there.

We all know that, but I haven't.

Not for a long time.

When I survived, I just thought, I'm just happy to be here.

But that sheet changed.

Changing.

Change.

I'm still absolutely happy to be here.

But I no longer want to act like a guy who is happy he just exists.

I want to act like a person who is happy he exists and takes care of himself to the level that proves he's earned survival.

I don't think Doritos are all that addictive.

Yes, it is.

But I know caffeine sure is.

And I've got it down to just about zero.

I don't think I consume any, but maybe it's somewhere in something.

This is what an old(ish) musings columns gives you.

And I hope you learned something.

You don't put 85 octane in a premium vehicle.

Do you?

I don't know.

But what I'm saying is take care of yourself better than you are.

Changing a habit is doable.

You can give up things you "love" to improve the things that make you love.

And I think you should.

But hey, that's just me musing.

And if I can give up the Ritos and 0, you can too.

Your figurative Ritos and 0.

You get me.

Do it!

I triple dog dare you.
