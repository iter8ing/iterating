Tilt The Picture, It's A Little Crooked

What's the luckiest moment of your life?

You don't have to tell me.

But I'd like you to think about it.

I'm not talking accomplishments.

I'm talking luck.

Something that happens seemingly by pure chance.

Not by skill.

Hard work.

Or strategy.

Just plain old magic.

Do you believe in magic?

I do.

Can you believe in God and magic?

I dunno.

But I do.

What is your luckiest moment?

What are your five luckiest moments?

Ten?

How high can you go?

How lucky have you been?

How lucky are you?

I want to learn to reframe.

Reframing is looking at the same thing from a different perspective.

I'm not (just) an old(ish) curmudgeon.

🖼

I'm lucky to be alive.

You are, too.

You are, too.

For the next day, week, life, act as if you're the luckiest person in the world.

See what happens.
