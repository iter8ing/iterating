Iterating

What is it that they say about idle hands?

Well, it's true.

Your hands always find something to do.

And if it ain't productive, well...

It'll play.

I got an honest question for you:

How much of your life is input?

And how much is output?

Meaning, how much of your day is absorbing data?

And how much of it is you producing it?

I admit, too much of my day is consuming.

Not enough compute.

I used to believe that was a point of achievement.

Now, I'm ashamed I ever thought it.

The trick is, such as tricks go, is to figure out what to output.

I don't think it's a time in thing.

The 10,000 hours thing.

I think that thought has changed to 10,000 iterations.

Iterating 10,000 times.

But what do you iterate?

There's a timeline where everyone has enough of their needs met that they're at the top of the hierarchy.

Focusing on building love, potential, and achieving it.

Beyond self.

Some people have it now.

How would you spend your days?

Those 40+ hours given back?

Would your hands do it with all thy might?

Or would they be idle?

Pick something.

Hopefully beneficial to yourself and society.

And work on it.

10,000 times.

Like a game of Telephone, the end will be nothing like the beginning.

But I think, I hope, that's the point.

The point isn't really becoming a "success."

It's whether or not you picked something productive to do with your hands.

And spent a lifetime perfecting it.
