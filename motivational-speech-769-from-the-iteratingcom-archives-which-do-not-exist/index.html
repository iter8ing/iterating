Motivational Speech #769 (From the iterating.com Archives Which Do Not Exist)

You never correct a fool.

You ever hear that?

Never.

Want to know why?

Because the fool will get angry.

Angrier.

Generally fools get angry easy.

Grrr.

And, with correction, they only get angrier.

Bitter(er).

Now, as a guy who likes to correct, who's left?

Non-fools.

Those who are willing to learn.

To be corrected.

Where warranted of course.

Correct those lovely folks, please.

Unless you're a fool.

Which, let's face it, you might be.

Statistically quite likely.

Being corrected by a fool is next level sheet.

Ever have that happen?

Final level.

But you.

You?

You're doing great.

Wouldn't change a thing!

Keep up the great work!
